Posts can belong to a series. Blowfish shows a series box at the top of each post in the series, linking the parts in order.

## Pages

Pages like **About** live directly in `content/`. They use the same editor as posts but aren't listed on the Posts page.

To add a page, open the **Pages** collection and choose **Add → Page**. Add it to the menu in **Site settings → menus.en.yaml**.
