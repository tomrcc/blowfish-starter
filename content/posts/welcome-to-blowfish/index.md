This site is a [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page) theme running on [CloudCannon](https://cloudcannon.com). Every page, post and setting you see can be edited without code.

## Three places to edit

- **The Visual Editor.** Open any page, click its text and type. The home page's heading, intro, buttons and image are all editable in place.
- **Posts and pages.** Use the Posts and Pages collections in the sidebar to write in the Content Editor, with the post's settings alongside.
- **Site settings.** The theme's colour scheme, header style, home page options, menus and author profile are all in the Site collection.

## Your first change

Try switching the colour scheme: open **Site settings → params.yaml**, pick a different scheme and save. The whole site restyles on the next build.
