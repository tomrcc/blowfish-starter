## What this site is

A starter template for [CloudCannon](https://cloudcannon.com) built on [Blowfish](https://blowfish.page), a Hugo theme. Everything the theme can do is still available; CloudCannon adds a friendly way to edit it.

## Getting in touch

Update the links under the author profile in **Site settings → languages.en.yaml**, and they'll appear wherever Blowfish shows your profile.
